## How to read these files

- **Stated** means the site or a Maven page says it.
- **Inferred** means it's my reading of the evidence and needs checking.
- Claims are NotLinear's own. I didn't check them against outside sources.
- Dates, prices, and ratings will go out of date, so check them before reusing them.
