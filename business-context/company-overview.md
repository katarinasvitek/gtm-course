## Founder

Pietro Montaldo is the founder and the face of the brand. He teaches the workshops and courses himself.

- Lecturer in applied AI at ESADE Business School
- Previously at Bocconi, Project A, and realy.app
- Scaled a software company from zero to $1M in under nine months
- Co-founded an AI orchestration startup acquired by Htech Group (Maven bio)
- Author of *Building Claude Skills* (Packt), described as a #1 Amazon Best Seller
- Grew LinkedIn from 500 to 35,000 followers and Substack from 0 to 7,000 subscribers in 18 months (Maven bio)

## Business model

**Stated:**

- **Team training:** custom programs, scoped on a 30-minute call booked through Cal.com
- **Live courses:** cohort-based, sold and paid for on Maven
- **Free workshops:** live online sessions, with sign-up on Maven
- **Newsletter:** roughly weekly workshop invites and AI playbooks, on Substack

**Inferred:** these work as a ladder. Free workshops and content build trust, courses prove the method to individuals, and those individuals can bring NotLinear into their companies. The custom team program is the site's main call to action and looks like the highest-value offer, though no prices are published.
