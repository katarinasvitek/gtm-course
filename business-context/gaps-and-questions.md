## Messaging tensions

- **"Non-technical" and Claude Code:** the certification and masterclass exclude Claude Code, while the bootcamp teaches Claude Code and GitHub. A non-technical buyer may need clearer guidance on that step up.
- **Multi-platform and Claude-focused:** the site lists Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and n8n, but every listed course and most workshops center on Claude. A company standardized on Copilot or ChatGPT may not see itself here. Copilot is named in the problem section but not in the tools list.
- **Teams and individuals:** the homepage leads with teams, but almost all the detail and proof is about individual courses. There's one team case study and no team training page.

## Questions a website can't answer

### Revenue and customers

1. What is the revenue split between team training and courses?
2. What does a typical team program cost, and how long does it run?
3. How many team deals close per month, and how long does the sales cycle take?
4. Which customers are the best fit, and which were a poor fit?

### Go-to-market

5. Where do booked calls actually come from: LinkedIn, Substack, Maven, workshops, or referrals?
6. How many course students or workshop attendees later bring in a team deal?
7. How well do free workshops convert into paid courses?
8. Which competitors come up on sales calls?

### Direction

9. What's the main goal for the next 6–12 months?
10. Is the aim to move further into enterprise team training, or to grow courses?
11. How much depends on Pietro personally, and can other trainers deliver the programs?
