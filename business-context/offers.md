## Claude for Non-Technical Operators: Advanced Certification

[Course page](https://maven.com/pietro-montaldo/claudefornontech) · Rated 4.9 from 82 reviews

- **For:** marketers, operators, and founders who want to go beyond chat, and people switching from ChatGPT or Gemini
- **Not for:** anyone who needs Claude Code, or anyone without a paid Claude plan and basic prompting experience
- **Format:** four live sessions, about 40% theory and 60% practical, with optional office hours
- **Topics:** the Claude ecosystem, Cowork setup, Skills and connectors, plugins and subagents, Claude Design and Artifacts
- **Includes:** a 1:1 session with Pietro, recordings, lifetime access, peer community, resource database, certificate

## AI Growth Systems Bootcamp: Automate Your GTM

[Course page](https://maven.com/pietro-montaldo/ai-growth-system-bootcamp-claude) · Rated 5.0 from 30 reviews

- **For:** business owners and independent experts, scale-up operators who own pipeline, and career changers
- **Format:** 4 weeks with 5 live sessions and 4 office hours, about 20% theory and 80% building
- **What people build:**
  1. A GTM context layer
  2. An outbound agent that finds intent signals, scores leads against the ICP, and drafts outreach
  3. An inbound agent that sorts and scores leads, drafts replies, and handles CRM logging
- **Also covers:** Claude Code and GitHub setup, a portfolio repo, and scheduling agents with Claude Routines
- **Includes:** a 1:1 implementation session, $1,500 of tool credits (Clay, Apify, Granola), templates, certificate, community, lifetime access, and a free retake in a later cohort
- **Prerequisite:** people new to Cowork and Skills are pointed to the Claude for Non-Technical Operators course first

## Claude Skills Masterclass

[Course page](https://maven.com/pietro-montaldo/claudeskills) · Rated 4.8 from 4 reviews

- **For:** freelancers, founders, operators, and marketers who already use Claude
- **Not for:** developers, as it doesn't cover Claude Code
- **Format:** about 2.5 hours live plus 1 hour of Q&A office hours (the site calls it half-day)
- **Topics:** creating skills, testing and evaluating them, subagents, connectors, and sharing skills across a team
