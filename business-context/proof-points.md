## Recurring review themes

- Practical, with little theory
- Clear explanations of complex topics, including for people with no technical background
- Generous with time through office hours, Q&A, and 1:1s
- Up to date in a fast-moving field
- Useful even to people who already considered themselves advanced
- Repeat customers, such as Moira (Fur - Ever) and Elsa (CMCO), who have taken several courses

## Critical feedback

- The pace is fast and the content dense, so some people need recordings to keep up.
- One reviewer wanted more use cases, and another wanted more breaks.
